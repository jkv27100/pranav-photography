About

Hi, I’m Pranav.

I’m a photographer based in Dublin, working across portrait, editorial and family photography. I’m drawn to quiet, honest moments — the look just before a smile, the light that only lasts a minute, the small gestures that say more than a pose ever could.

Every session starts as a conversation, not a checklist. Whether it’s a studio portrait, an editorial concept, or an afternoon with your family, my aim is the same: photographs that still feel true to you years from now.
